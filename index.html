About Us

Restoring Purity, One Tank at a Time.

Cleanin services was founded on a simple premise: Clean water is not a luxury; it is a fundamental right. With over a decade of experience, we have revolutionized the water tank cleaning industry by replacing manual, unhygienic methods with state-of-the-art automation.

We are ISO 9001:2015 certified and serve residential complexes, corporate parks, and industrial units. Our commitment goes beyond cleaning; we provide comprehensive water storage health assessments to ensure your family and employees are safe from waterborne diseases.
