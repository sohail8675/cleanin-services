Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers!

How long does tank cleaning take?

For residential tanks (500-2000 litres), cleaning takes about 45-60 minutes. For larger commercial or industrial tanks, it may take 2-3 hours depending on the size and condition.

Is the cleaning process safe?

Yes, absolutely! We use food-grade, eco-friendly cleaning agents that are completely safe for drinking water. Our UV sterilization ensures 99.9% germ-free water.

How often should I clean my water tank?

We recommend cleaning your water tank every 6 months for residential use. For commercial establishments, quarterly cleaning is advisable to maintain hygiene standards.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all payment methods including Cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, and Bank Transfer. Payment is collected after service completion.

Do you provide service in my area?

We currently serve 50+ cities across India. Enter your location during booking, and our system will confirm if we have coverage in your area.
